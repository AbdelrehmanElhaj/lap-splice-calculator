Lap schedule by bar diameter and concrete strength

  • Hook columns use the hook settings from the calculator.
  • N/P: tension lap splices are not permitted for bars larger than Ø36. Use mechanical or welded splices.
  • Tension values take Ktr = 0, equal bar sizes and no 1.25 fy. Use the calculator to take credit for transverse steel or to handle mixed sizes.
  • For fy above 550 MPa, ACI 318-25 also requires the compression lap to be at least the tension lap. Take the longer tabulated value.

Basis and detailing notes

  1. SBC 304-18 is based on ACI 318-14, so its column omits provisions added in ACI 318-19 and 318-25: the grade factor ψg, the minimum Ktr for high-strength bars, and the compression lap rule for fy above 550 MPa.
  2. Tension development: ld = fy·ψt·ψe·ψs·ψg / (1.1·λ·√f'c·(cb+Ktr)/db) · db. The lap is based on ld without the 300 mm minimum and without the excess-steel reduction.
  3. cb is the lesser of clear cover plus db/2 and half the centre-to-centre spacing of the spliced bars.
  4. Class A applies only when As provided / As required is at least 2.0 and no more than 50% of the bars are spliced within the lap. Otherwise Class B.
  5. Different bar sizes in tension: the greater of ld of the larger bar and the lap of the smaller bar. In compression: the greater of ldc of the larger bar and the compression lap of the smaller bar.
  6. Non-contact laps in flexural members: transverse spacing of spliced bars no more than the lesser of one-fifth of the lap and 150 mm.
  7. Bundled bars and the column tie or spiral reductions for compression laps (0.83 and 0.75) are not calculated here.
  8. Seismic, special moment frame beams: no laps within joints, within 2h of the joint face, or within 2h of sections where flexural yielding is expected. Hoops over the whole lap at no more than the lesser of d/4 and 100 mm.
  9. Seismic, special moment frame columns: laps only within the centre half of the member length, designed as tension laps and enclosed by confining transverse reinforcement.
  10. Seismic, special structural walls: where longitudinal bars are likely to yield, develop 1.25 fy. ACI 318-19 and 318-25 also restrict laps of boundary bars near critical sections.
  11. Standard hooks: ACI 318-25 returns to the ACI 318-14 form, ldh = 0.24·fy·ψe·ψs·ψcc·ψr / (λ·√f'c) · db, and adds a bar size factor ψs for large bars. SBC 304-18 uses the same form without ψs. Minimum 8db and 150 mm. ψcc and ψr may always be taken as 1.0.
  12. Hooks in special moment frame joints: ldh = fy·db / (5.4·λ·√f'c) for Ø10 to Ø36, with the hook inside the confined joint core. Straight bars in the joint need 2.5 ldh, or 3.25 ldh for top bars.
  13. Hook geometry: inside bend diameter 6db up to Ø25, 8db for Ø28 to Ø36, 10db above. Extension 12db for a 90° hook; the greater of 4db and 65 mm for a 180° hook.
  14. Mechanical splices: ACI 318-25 uses Class L, G and S in place of Type 1 and Type 2, which SBC 304-18 still uses.
